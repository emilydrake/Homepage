A Rant About "Technology" Its technology is how a society copes with physical reality: how people get and keep and cook food, how they clothe themselves, what their power sources are (animal? human? water? wind? electricity? other?) what they build with and what they build, their medicine - and so on and on. Perhaps very ethereal people aren't interested in these mundane, bodily matters, but I'm fascinated by them, and I think most of my readers are too. I thought this little chunk of the arcticle was very interesting and reminded me the true meaning of technology, the application of scientific knowledge for practical purposes. Its so often used along side objects with screens and wires, you often forget to acknowledge the technology in your everyday life. Considering the different ways we can learn from technology makes you take a second look at things around you. I appreciated the argument and thought it was a concise piece that was a thoughtful reminder. My website is a shifting house next to a river of knowledge. What could yours be? A Handmade Web Article argues the term handmade web refers to web pages coded by hand rather than by software. After reading this article it made me think about the care and precision that goes into a handmade website, especially when the author compared them to a handmade book, zine, or piece of art. Today, with the rise of AI websites are all looking more and more similar, so I think it’s good to acknowledge the importance of something more personal. I enjoyed the article overall because I think everyone should see how there is still value in making something yourself, even its its not perfect or not seen by many people.
